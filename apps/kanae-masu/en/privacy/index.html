Privacy Policy

App: Kanae Masu (叶えマス) - 100 Things To Do In Life
Effective: November 5, 2026

1. Information we collect and store

The app uses the information you enter, such as wish titles, notes, categories, deadlines, achievement dates, preparation items, your birthday, and photos you take or choose, to provide the board, widgets, notifications and other features. This information is stored on your device. No account registration is required, and the operator does not collect your personal information on its own servers.

If you turn on iCloud sync (it is off by default), your data, including photos, is synced between devices that use the same Apple Account through iCloud (CloudKit) provided by Apple. The data is stored only in your own iCloud and the operator cannot access it. There is no feature for sharing with other users.

2. Photos and camera

To fill a cell with a photo when you achieve a wish, you can take a photo with the camera or choose one from your photo library. Taking a photo requires camera permission. A chosen photo is copied and stored inside the app, and it is never sent to the operator.

3. Notifications

Notifications for deadlines, letters and the year-end reminder are shown only if you allow them. They are local notifications scheduled on your device, and no information is sent to any server for them. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Features that work on your device

You can lock the app with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. Biometric data is handled by Apple's system, and neither the app nor the operator obtains it. Widgets, Spotlight search, and Siri and Shortcuts work using the data on your device. Files you export with the backup feature are under your own control.

5. Advertising and third-party services

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (Google AdMob) to show interstitial (full-screen) ads in the free version. The ad provider may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), ad data, usage data, diagnostics, and approximate location for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. What is actually collected depends on your consent, device settings, the ad configuration and Google's service specifications.

To personalize ads, the app may ask for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency. You can keep using the app if you decline. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

In the EEA, the UK and some other regions, Google's messaging tool asks for your consent about ads. For details, see the Google Privacy Policy.

6. In-app purchases

The app offers an in-app purchase to add more than 100 wishes and hide ads. Apple processes the purchase, and the operator does not obtain your payment information such as card numbers. The app uses Apple's StoreKit to check your purchase status.

7. How we use information

Information that is collected or processed is used to provide the app's features, to sync between devices, to deliver and measure ads, to prevent abuse, and to maintain quality and safety.

8. Retention and deletion

Data on your device is kept until you delete a wish, use Reset Data in Settings, or delete the app. Deleted wishes stay in "Recently Deleted" for 30 days and are then removed permanently. Data synced to iCloud is managed according to your iCloud settings and Apple's policies, and can be deleted in iOS Settings.

The retention and deletion of information handled by advertising providers follow each provider's policy.

9. Your choices

You can turn iCloud sync on or off in the app's Settings. You can change camera, notification, and tracking permissions at any time in iOS Settings. In the EEA, the UK and some other regions, you can review your ad consent from the app's settings.

10. Security

The operator makes reasonable efforts to protect the information handled by the app against unauthorized access, leakage, alteration and loss. For third-party services, we review each provider's security policies.

11. Children

The app is not primarily directed to children. If you are of an age that requires parental consent under applicable law, please use the app with your parent's or guardian's consent.

12. Changes

This policy may be revised to reflect changes in features, laws or third-party services. We will notify you in the app or on this site of any significant changes.

13. Contact

For questions about this policy or requests about your data, please use the contact form.
