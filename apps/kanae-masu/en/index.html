Kanae Masu (叶えマス) - 100 Things To Do In Life

Line up the things you want to do in life on a 100-cell board, and fill each cell with a photo when you make it happen. You can also record deadlines and the preparation each wish needs.

FAQ

Does it cost anything?

The basic features are free. An optional in-app purchase lets you add more than 100 wishes and hide ads. Please see the App Store for what is included and the price.

How do I add a wish?

Tap the add button on the board and enter a title, category, deadline and so on. New wishes go into an empty cell.

What do I do when a wish comes true?

Open the wish, mark it as achieved, and take a photo or pick one from your library. The cell is then filled with your photo. You can also record things you achieved in the past by setting the date.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap "+", choose "Kanae Masu", and pick a size. iOS decides when widgets refresh, so changes can take a little while to appear.

Where is my data stored? Can I use it on other devices?

Your data is stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings (it is off by default), your data is synced between your devices through iCloud (CloudKit) under your own Apple Account. No account registration is required.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Turn on iCloud sync, or use the backup feature in Settings to export your data (including photos) and import it on the new device.

I deleted a wish by mistake.

Deleted wishes are kept in "Recently Deleted" for 30 days, and you can restore them during that time.

How do I erase all my data?

Use Reset Data in Settings to delete your wishes, your settings, or everything. Deleting the app removes the data on your device. Data stored in iCloud can be removed in iOS Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage.

I'm not getting notifications.

Deadline and year-end reminders are delivered only if you allowed notifications. Check iOS Settings → Kanae Masu → Notifications.

About ads

The free version may show ads. In the EEA, the UK and some other regions you can review your ad consent from the app's settings. You can also change the tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later.
