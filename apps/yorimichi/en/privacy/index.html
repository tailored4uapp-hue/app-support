Privacy Policy

App: Yorimichi (ヨリミチ)
Effective: October 26, 2026

1. Information we collect and store

The app uses the information you enter, such as list names, items, quantities, notes, photos, due dates, store choices and notification settings, to provide your shopping lists and notifications. This information is stored on your device. No account registration is required, and the operator does not collect your personal information on its own servers.

Settings such as your home location and radius and the notification pause state are stored only on this iPhone and are not sent to iCloud.

2. Use of location and motion data

The app uses your device's location to detect when you are near a store on your lists and to notify you. To notify you while the app is closed, Location must be set to "Always". Location is used only for on-device decisions and is not sent to the operator's servers.

To find stores, your search words and the area around your current location are sent to Apple Maps search (MapKit). This applies to "Anywhere" lists as well as searching for a store for a "One store" list, setting your home, and searching for stores to exclude. Apple's privacy policy applies to this communication. The operator does not receive this data.

When you tap "Get Directions", the store name and location are passed to Apple Maps to show the route.

To avoid notifying you while you ride a train or car, the app uses your device's Motion & Fitness data (to tell how you are moving). This is processed on your device and is not sent anywhere.

You can change access to Location, Motion & Fitness, Notifications, Camera and Photos at any time in iOS Settings.

3. Sync with iCloud

If you turn on iCloud sync, your lists and items (including photos) are synced between devices that use the same Apple Account through iCloud (CloudKit). The data is stored only in your own iCloud, and the operator cannot access it. There is no feature for sharing with other users.

4. Advertising and third-party services

The app does not use third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics or tracking. It does not use App Tracking Transparency.

5. In-app purchase

The app has a one-time in-app purchase. Apple's App Store (StoreKit) processes the purchase and checks its status. The operator does not receive payment information such as credit card numbers.

6. How we use information

Information is used to provide the app's features, to notify you near stores, to sync across your devices, and to maintain quality and safety.

7. Retention and deletion

Deleted lists and items are kept in the Trash for 30 days and then permanently deleted. Deleting from the Trash also removes them from your other devices synced through iCloud. Deleting the app removes the data on your device. Data synced to iCloud can be removed in iOS Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage.

8. Security

The operator makes reasonable efforts to protect the information handled by the app against unauthorized access, leakage, alteration and loss. Data storage and sync rely on iOS and iCloud.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children. If you are of an age that requires parental consent under applicable law, please use the app with a parent or guardian's consent.

10. Changes

This policy may be revised as features, laws or third-party services change. We will announce important changes in the app or on this site.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy or requests about your data, please use the contact form.
