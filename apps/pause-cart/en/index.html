Pause Cart

Add what you want to buy, wait a little, then decide to buy it or skip it. A simple app to curb impulse buying and keep a shopping list. In Japan the app is called 「ちょいまち」.

FAQ

Does it cost anything?

The app is free. There are no in-app purchases. A full-screen ad is shown after every 3 buy/skip decisions.

How does it work?

Add an item with its name, price, waiting days, a note, and a photo. When the waiting period ends, choose whether to buy it or skip it. To help you decide, the app can convert the price into hours of work using your hourly wage, and show its future value based on an expected annual return.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap "+", choose "Pause Cart", and pick a size. iOS decides when widgets refresh, so changes can take a little while to appear.

Can I add items from other apps or Safari?

Choose "Pause Cart" in the Share menu to save the product you are looking at. You can also add items with Siri or Shortcuts.

Will I get notifications?

Yes. You get a reminder when the waiting period is about to end. The app asks for permission the first time, and you can change it any time in iOS Settings.

Where is my data stored?

Your entries are stored on your device. No account is required and nothing is sent to the operator's servers. There is no automatic sync to other devices.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete each item inside the app. Deleting the app removes the data on your device.

How do I change my ad consent?

In the EEA, the UK and some other regions, you can review your ad consent from "Ad privacy choices" in the app's settings. Without your consent, only non-personalized ads are shown. The same applies if you do not allow tracking in iOS Settings.

Which devices and languages are supported?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The app is available in Japanese, English, Korean, Traditional and Simplified Chinese, Spanish, French, German, and Brazilian Portuguese. It is not distributed in mainland China.
