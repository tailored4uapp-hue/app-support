Privacy Policy

App: Pause Cart (Japanese name: ちょいまち)
Effective date: November 2, 2026

1. Information we handle

The app uses the information you enter, such as item names, prices, waiting days, notes, photos, URLs, your hourly wage and expected return settings, and your buy/skip history, to provide the waiting-period countdown, conversions, and widget features. This information is stored on your device. No account registration is required, and the operator does not collect your personal information on any server of its own. The app has no feature for syncing to other devices or sharing data with other users.

2. Use of device features

Depending on your actions, the app uses the following features. None of them send information to the operator.

  • Camera and photo library: to add a picture of an item.
  • Notifications: to remind you when a waiting period ends.
  • Widgets: to show part of the on-device data on your Home Screen.
  • Share extension: to receive product information from other apps and save it.
  • Siri and Shortcuts (App Intents): to add items by voice or shortcut.

3. Advertising and third-party services

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (Google AdMob) and shows a full-screen (interstitial) ad after every 3 buy/skip decisions. The ad provider may collect device identifiers, advertising data, usage data, diagnostic information, and approximate location for purposes such as serving and measuring ads and preventing fraud. What is actually collected depends on your consent, your device settings, the ad configuration, and Google's service specifications.

In the EEA, the UK and some other regions, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for your consent regarding ads. The app may also ask for permission to track you through Apple's App Tracking Transparency. Without consent or permission, only non-personalized ads are shown. For details, see the Google Privacy Policy.

4. In-app purchases

The app has no in-app purchases.

5. Purposes of use

Information is used to provide app features, serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and maintain quality and security.

6. Retention and deletion

Data on your device is kept until you delete the item or delete the app. Retention and deletion of information handled by ad providers follow each provider's policies.

7. Your choices

You can change camera, photo, notification, and tracking permissions in your device settings. You can change your ad consent from the consent screen or from "Ad privacy choices" in the app's settings (shown only in regions where consent is required).

8. Security

The operator makes reasonable efforts to protect information handled by the app against unauthorized access, leakage, alteration, and loss. For third-party services, we review each provider's security policies.

9. Children

The app is not primarily directed at children. If you are of an age that requires parental consent under applicable law, please use the app with your parent's or guardian's consent.

10. Changes

This policy may be revised in response to changes in features, laws, or third-party services. If there is an important change, we will notify you in the app or on this site.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy or requests regarding data deletion, please use the contact form.
